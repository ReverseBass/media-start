// first time? set up in a few minutes

  1. Accept the Tailscale invite. Open the invite email from Tailscale and accept it. Sign in with Google, Apple, Microsoft or GitHub.
  2. Install Tailscale on each device you'll use. Get it from tailscale.com/download. It's free and works on phones, computers, Apple TV and Android TV. Sign in with the account you accepted the invite with.
  3. Turn Tailscale on. Then come back to this page. The bar above turns green when you're connected.

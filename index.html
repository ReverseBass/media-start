// first time? setup

  1. Accept the Tailscale invite. It comes in a separate email from Tailscale. Tailscale is a private network that connects you to VAULT (similar to a VPN you have to connect to when you work from home).
  2. Install Tailscale on each device you'll use. Sign in with the account you accepted the invite with.
  3. Turn Tailscale on.
  4. Open vault.rusu.fyi. You're here. The bar at the top checks your connection and turns green when you're connected.
  5. Sign in to each app with the username and password from your welcome email.
  6. Enjoy!

// how to use

  • watch: open Watch, sign in, and pick something to play. Your progress is saved, so you can stop and pick up later on another device.
  • request: want a movie or show that isn't in Jellyfin yet? Open Request, search for it, and click Request. It shows up in Jellyfin once it's ready.
  • read: open Read to read books in your browser. Your place is saved between devices.

// tips

  • On a phone or TV, install the Jellyfin app and enter this server address:
    https://watch.rusu.fyi
  • To watch on a TV, mirror your phone's screen with AirPlay or Chromecast. For better quality, install Tailscale and the Jellyfin app on the TV itself (Apple TV, Google TV, Android TV).
  • Requests usually show up in Jellyfin within 15 minutes.
  • You can request up to 5 movies and 5 shows per week. Each season of a show counts as one.
  • If an app won't load, check that Tailscale is switched on.

// privacy

Other users can't see what you watch, read or request, but I can as the admin. I won't go looking, but you should know it's possible.

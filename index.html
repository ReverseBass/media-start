First time? Set up in a few minutes

  1. Install Tailscale on your device. Get it from tailscale.com/download. It's free and works on phones, computers, Apple TV and Android TV.
  2. Open the invite link you were sent and accept it. Sign in with Google, Apple, Microsoft or GitHub. Use the same account in the Tailscale app.
  3. Switch Tailscale on. Then come back to this page. The bar above turns green when you're connected.
